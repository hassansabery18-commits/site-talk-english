# B1-16 | Cut and fill in simple words | القطع والردم بكلام بسيط
ENG Omar, new trainee engineer, am_liam
SCENE: مهندس متدرب جديد بيسألك يعني إيه قطع وردم.
ENG: Hassan, what does cut and fill mean?
QS: Cut means we remove soil from high areas.
ENG: And fill?
QS: Fill means we put soil in low areas.
ENG: Where does the fill material come from?
QS: Usually from the cut. We use the good material again.
ENG: So we balance them?
QS: Exactly. A good balance saves time and money.
ENG: That's clear now. Thank you.
P: what does it mean? = يعني إيه؟
P: we remove soil = بنشيل تراب
P: high areas / low areas = الأماكن العالية / الواطية
P: we use it again = بنستخدمه تاني
P: saves time and money = بيوفر وقت وفلوس
H: remove = ri-MOOV — رِيمُوف
H: balance = BAL-ans — بالَنس
H: exactly = ig-ZAKT-lee — إِجْزاكْتْلِي
R: Cut means we remove soil from high areas.
R: Fill means we put soil in low areas.
R: Usually the fill material comes from the cut.
R: We use the good material again.
R: A good balance saves time and money.
T: Explain cut and fill to a new trainee. | اشرح القطع والردم لمتدرب جديد. | Cut means we remove soil from the high areas. Fill means we put soil in the low areas. Usually we use the cut material for the fill. | cut means; fill means; material
T: Explain why balancing cut and fill is important. | اشرح ليه موازنة القطع والردم مهمة. | If we balance cut and fill, we don't need to buy or remove a lot of soil. A good balance saves time and money. | balance; saves time; money
